CHORDLAB bundles 21 unmodified MP3 files from the Tone.js distribution, spanning C2–C7 in minor thirds. The upstream README is retained as `SOURCE-README.txt`; it labels the collection V2 and includes a V3 changelog. File hashes and the exact source revision are recorded in `manifest.json`.

This is a compact, single-layer subset of the recordings, not the full multisampled SFZ instrument. During playback, CHORDLAB repitches neighboring recorded notes, normalizes their playback gain, and applies attack/release envelopes. It does not include the original library's multiple velocity layers, pedal/hammer noise or sympathetic-resonance layers. The bundled MP3 files themselves have not been edited.
